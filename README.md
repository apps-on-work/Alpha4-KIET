# Alpha4-KIET
It is a website that gives you collective info of your campus, what's happening around giving a new  guide to freshers.


## Club Predictor 

This is an AI tool built to help students figure out which technical club to join. In a college with dozens of different societies—covering everything from cybersecurity to open-source—it’s easy to get confused or pick the wrong one. Alpha4 lets you just type in what you’re interested in using your own words. It then suggests the 3 clubs that actually match your interests, helping you find your circle without the stress of scrolling through lists.


## EVENTS 

The Events page is a centralized spot for everything happening on campus. We use this to track hackathons, tech workshops, and—most importantly—club recruitments. Usually, recruitment dates and interview tasks get lost in flooded WhatsApp groups or random Instagram stories. This page puts all those updates in one place. Whether a club is looking for new members or a big competition is coming up, you’ll find the dates and links here so you don’t miss out. 


## 101 & MAP 

101's a place where you can find study notes for your subjects and guides on how things actually work on campus, and we are planning to add guides and other features soon like how to handle registration or where to get certain permissions and important documents. The Map feature gives you a digital layout of the entire campus so you can find exactly where you need to go. It covers everything from lecture halls and canteens to administrative offices.
